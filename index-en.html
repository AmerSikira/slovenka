THREE STEPS TO WORKING TOGETHER

A clear path from idea to clothing.

You do not need every answer for our first conversation. Tell us what you want to make; we work through the remaining details together.

  1. 01

    Project review

    Share your garment type, styles, quantities and target timeline. We review your documentation and production possibilities.

  2. 02

    Scope agreement

    We agree materials, technical requirements, responsibilities and timing. Samples, approvals and additional services are included as agreed.

  3. 03

    Production & handover

    We make clothing to the confirmed scope, with the agreed checks and handover arrangements.
